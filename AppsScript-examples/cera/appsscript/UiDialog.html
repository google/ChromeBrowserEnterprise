Manual Export & Staging Checklist

Follow these 2 essential steps from the CERA Welcome Guide to retrieve and stage raw browser logs

🔒 Need Administrator Assistance?
If you do not have Google Admin Console access, ask your administrator to enable the 3 Chrome Enterprise pre-requisite policies below and export the Chrome audit log sheets to Google Drive for you:
1. Event reporting (Enable all (Optional: Password breach, Login)) — admin.google.com/.../on_security_event
2. Managed browser reporting (Enable) — admin.google.com/.../cloud_reporting
3. Managed profile reporting (Enable) — admin.google.com/.../cloud_profile_reporting
1
Export Chrome Log Events from Google Admin Console
Retrieve organizational browser telemetry from the Workspace Audit & Investigation console.
Step 1 of 2
🔗 Open Admin Console Audit: goo.gle/cera-logs ↗
Navigation Path: Admin Console ➔ Security ➔ Reports ➔ Audit and investigation ➔ Chrome log events
•
Date range: export the last 7 days. A 7-day sample shows how data is shared in your organization; the full history is not needed.
•
Select Event Types: Ensure all primary threat vectors are selected: file upload, download, print, password reuse, and malware transfer.
•
Trigger Export: Click the Export button in the top toolbar and select Google Sheets (or CSV).
•
Export language: Exports in English, Japanese (日本語), Korean (한국어), Simplified Chinese (简体中文), and Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) are automatically normalized. If your Google Account uses another language, switch to English at myaccount.google.com/language before exporting, or use Direct Ingestion.
⏳ Notice: Large Exports Take Time
The export can take a few minutes or longer, depending on the number of events. You can close this dialog now and come back when the export is in your Drive.
